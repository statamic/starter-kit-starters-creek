We started Starter’s Creek with a simple idea: ordinary life is boring. Living an extraordinary life means letting go what people think about you.

## What you’ll find here

We write literally whatever comes to our mind, no matter how ridiculous, nonsensical, full of nonsequiturs, or antithetical to whatever it takes to be "popular".

## Pull up a chair

Read everything. Read it backwards, forwards, sideways, and in the wrong order. Pretend it says something else. It's totally fine if you find yourself up Starter’s Creek without a paddle.
